Principle

Our policies are carefully set to ensure that the TikTok platform remains a safe and healthy space for its diverse community. We aim to create an environment where users can take part in a positive community experience.

Accounting and taxation

Ad content and landing pages advertising services related to tax planning, tax accounting, and tax declaration are generally allowed.

Market-specific requirements

Accounting and taxation content is allowed with restrictions in the following markets:

Argentina, Australia, Austria, Bahrain, Belarus, Belgium, Brazil, Bulgaria, Cambodia, Chile, Colombia, Czech Republic, Denmark, Ecuador, Egypt, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Indonesia, Iraq, Ireland, Israel, Italy, Japan, Jordan, Kazakhstan, Kuwait, Lebanon, Luxembourg, Macau, Malaysia, Mexico, Morocco, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Oman, Pakistan, Panama, Peru, Philippines, Poland, Portugal, Qatar, Romania, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, South Africa, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Taiwan, Thailand, United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom, Uruguay, Vietnam

Accounting and taxation content is not allowed in the following markets:

Albania, Brunei, Cyprus, Croatia, El Salvador, Estonia, Ethiopia, Honduras, Kyrgyzstan, Latvia, Laos, Libya, Lithuania, Mali, Mauritius, Monaco, Myanmar, Nicaragua, Senegal, Serbia, Slovakia, Slovenia, South Korea, Tanzania, Turkey, Uganda, Ukraine

Legal services and products

Ads and landing pages promoting legal services and products, including those offered by law firms, licensed professionals, legal counseling services, and legal-related applications, are allowed if certain requirements are met, or might not be allowed depending on the target market. See the Market-specific requirements section for more details.

As an advertiser, you are solely responsible for ensuring that your ads comply with all applicable laws, regulations, industry standards, ethical codes, and professional guidelines, including but not limited to those established by relevant regulatory bodies, institutions governing the legal profession, and advertising authorities.

What is allowed:

Educational content about any legal topic, as long as the ad is purely informative.

Market-specific requirements:

Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Dominican Republic, Ecuador, Guatemala, Mexico, Panama, Paraguay, Peru, Uruguay

Ads for legal services, such as law firms and licensed professionals, aimed to assist clients in legal matters are only allowed if the following requirements are met:

The ad must be restricted to audiences aged 18 years and older.

Do not offer legal consultation or representation related to industries that are not allowed under our advertising policies.

Do not promote or facilitate illegal, deceptive, or unethical behavior.

Ads for services related to criminal law or legal matters involving children are not allowed.

Ads for services related to family law, immigration law, employment law, personal injury lawsuits are not allowed.

Ads for legal products, such as tools, resources, or software that assist individuals or businesses in handling legal matters, such as legal books, legal software and legal courses are allowed if the following requirements are met:

Do not offer products related to industries that are restricted or not allowed under our advertising policies.

Do not promote or facilitate illegal, deceptive, or unethical behavior.

Canada, United States

Ads for legal services, such as law firms and licensed professionals, aimed to assist clients in legal matters are only allowed if the following requirements are met:

The ad must be restricted to audiences aged 18 years and older.

Do not offer legal consultation or representation related to industries that are not allowed under our advertising policies.

Do not promote or facilitate illegal, deceptive, or unethical behavior.

Ads for services related to family law, immigration law, employment law, personal injury lawsuits are not allowed.

Ads for legal products, such as tools, resources, or software that assist individuals or businesses in handling legal matters, such as legal books, legal software and legal courses are allowed if the following requirements are met:

Do not offer products related to industries that are restricted or not allowed under our advertising policies.

Do not promote or facilitate illegal, deceptive, or unethical behavior.

Media and entertainment

Ad content for movies, games and television must use age-targeting that is in line with market industry age certification requirements. You are responsible for ensuring that all advertising of your movie, game, or television show, complies with applicable local classification laws. This includes accurately applying the correct classification rating in accordance with the legal requirements relevant to your jurisdiction.

We do not allow insulting words or actions or gestures in ad content.

Jump scares, visuals of horror or supernatural characters may be used in ad creatives provided they appear after the first 6 seconds.

Market-specific requirements:

Brief or incidental references to and depictions of alcohol are allowed in film, television, and game trailers, if the ad is restricted to audiences aged 18 years and older, does not feature minors, all references are moderate and do not feature specific brands, and does not glamorize alcohol use and consumption in the following markets:

Albania, Austria, Bahrain, Belarus, Belgium, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Egypt, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Israel, Italy, Iraq, Jordan, Kazakhstan, Kuwait, Latvia, Lebanon, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Monaco, Morocco, Netherlands, Norway, Oman, Pakistan, Poland, Portugal, Qatar, Romania, Russia, Saudi Arabia, Slovakia, Slovenia, South Africa, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Turkey, Ukraine, United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom.

All mentions or clips of any films, TV shows, or video, computer, or phone game, regardless of age rating, are not allowed in the following markets:

Brunei, El Salvador, Ethiopia, Honduras, Kyrgyzstan, Laos, Libya, Mali, Mauritius, Myanmar, Nicaragua, Senegal, Tanzania, Uganda

Real estate

Businesses or organizations that carry out services related to land and buildings, including those engaged in the development, construction, management, marketing, and brokerage of real estate are allowed if the following requirements are met:

Comply with relevant laws and be registered and licensed with regional authorities.

Disclose advertiser information and contact details on landing pages.

Market-specific requirements:

Real estate agencies that engage in acquiring, renting, and leasing properties overseas are not allowed in Cyprus, Japan.

Ads promoting real estate services and investment franchise services are not allowed in Cyprus, South Africa.

Real Estate services related to land and buildings, including those engaged in the development, construction, management, marketing, leasing, and brokerage of real estate are not allowed in the following markets:

Albania, Brunei, El Salvador, Ethiopia, Honduras, Kyrgyzstan, Laos, Libya, Mali, Mauritius, Monaco, Myanmar, Nicaragua, Senegal, Tanzania, Uganda

Food and drinks high in fat, salt, or sugar (HFSS)

If your promoted brand or product is classified as HFSS according to local regulations in your target market, we require you to disclose this to us. For auction ads, you can declare HFSS status yourself when uploading a new ad group. For reservation ads, you should liaise with your TikTok representative.

Market-specific requirements

Ads for HFSS products must be restricted to audiences aged 18 years and older in the following markets:

Australia, Ireland, New Zealand, Norway, Portugal, United Kingdom

Ads for HFSS products are not allowed in the following markets:

Albania, Brunei, El Salvador, Ethiopia, Honduras, Kyrgyzstan, Laos, Libya, Mali, Mauritius, Monaco, Myanmar, Nicaragua, Senegal, Tanzania, Uganda

Ads for Less Healthy Foods (LHF) are not allowed in the following market:

United Kingdom

Note: When uploading an ad group, you must attest that you are in compliance with The Advertisting (Less Healthy Food Definitions and Exemptions) Regulations 2024.

Unsuitable business products or services

We do not allow the promotion of services and activities that are unsuitable, illegal, or that violate our Community Guidelines.

Examples of what is not allowed:

Abortion services

Hazardous chemical products

Modeling or influencer recruitment opportunities

Funeral services

Random, surprise, mystery, or blind boxes that claim to include luxury items or high-cost goods

Prediction Markets

Ads related to prediction markets or event outcome trading platforms may be allowed with restrictions in the following markets:

Canada, United States, Philippines, Bolivia, Ecuador, Guatemala, Dominican Republic, Paraguay, Colombia, Panama, Peru, United Kingdom, Ireland, Denmark, Finland, Sweden, Israel

Markets not listed above are currently not eligible for Prediction Market advertising.

Market-specific requirements

Ads for Prediction Markets may be allowed if the following requirements are met:

Work with a TikTok Sales Representative to obtain permission via an application process.

The ads must be age-targeted to the appropriate audience.

Comply with disclosure requirements, including disclaimers.

Examples of what may be allowed:

Platforms using financial trading or event contract terminology

Regulated event outcome trading platforms

Platforms where users trade contracts with other participants

Examples of what is not allowed:

Platforms that refer to users as "placing bets" or use gambling terminology

Odds-based or sportsbook-style betting products

Products not licensed or permitted as required by local regulation in the target market
